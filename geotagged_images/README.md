# Geotagged photographs

Photographs of the team at the project demonstration, stamped by the GPS Map Camera app with
the location, coordinates, date and time. Both were taken on Thursday 5 November 2026 at VESIT,
Chembur, Mumbai.

| [2026-11-05_team_at_the_demo_station.jpg](2026-11-05_team_at_the_demo_station.jpg) | 14:05 IST | The four team members at the demo station: the project posters behind them, the app on one laptop and the code on the other |

Location on both stamps: 19.0453° N, 72.8889° E, Collector Colony, Chembur, Mumbai 400074. The
town name the app printed on the stamp ("Kudal") is its own reverse-geocoding; the coordinates
and the postal address are the record, and both place the photographs on the VESIT campus.
