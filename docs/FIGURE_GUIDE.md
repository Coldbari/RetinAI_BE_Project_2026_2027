# Figure guide — what each graph shows, and the order to read them in

Generated by `scripts/make_paper_figures.py` (dark: `graphs/paper/`, print: `graphs/paper/light/`).
Every figure is drawn from a committed artifact under `results/rop/`; none of the numbers are
typed in, so a figure cannot drift away from the result it claims to show. Regenerate with:

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/make_paper_figures.py                 # screen / slides
FIG_THEME=light .venv/bin/python scripts/make_paper_figures.py # journal / print
```

---

## If you have five minutes

Read **04 → 10 → 14**, in that order. They are the three things this project actually found.

| | Figure | The one sentence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **04** score shift | A screening threshold fitted at one hospital called *every* image positive at another. |
| 2 | **10** evaluation unit | The same predictions score QWK 0.52 per image and 0.80 per eye. The unit was the problem, not the model. |
| 3 | **14** head equivalence | Our "better architecture" reversed sign when we changed the random seed. It was noise. |

Everything else is the evidence behind those three, or the fix that followed.

---

## A. What the data is

**01 · Inclusion flow.** 13,037 images acquired → 8,024 six-class eligible → three disjoint
sets. Exclusions are counted with reasons that sum to the drop; the generating script refuses
to emit a flow that does not balance. Note the three analysis sets add back to 8,024.

**02 · Corpus composition.** Class counts in the cross-validation pool, and the size of each
analysis set. The corpus is dominated by Normal, and Stage 4/5 barely exists — which is why
those classes are marked "not externally validated" everywhere rather than being quoted.

## B. What the model is

The architecture diagram lives in `images/rop_staging/architecture.png`
(`scripts/make_architecture_diagram.py`): one EfficientNetV2-S backbone feeding three heads —
an ordinal CORN stage head, an independent AP-ROP sigmoid branch, and a gradient-reversal site
adversary that is training-time machinery only. **Since 19 Aug 2026 this single model produces
the binary screening verdict, the ICROP stage, and the Grad-CAM.**

## C. The screening failure

**04 · Score shift.** *Read this one first.* Left: the retired ResNet50 at its own hospital,
where the 0.1933 threshold separates healthy from diseased reasonably. Right: the same model at
a hospital it never trained on. The entire distribution has moved above the line — the lowest
score of any image there is 0.279 — so all 663 images, including all 150 healthy eyes, are
flagged. Specificity 0.000. The threshold is not badly chosen; it is being applied to a
distribution it was not fitted to.

**05 · ROC transfer.** Both models on the *same* 663 external images, so the gap belongs to the
models and not to the test. Every model loses discrimination away from home (structured
0.970 → 0.821); the retired head lost most of it (0.691).

**06 · Threshold sweep.** Sensitivity and specificity against threshold at both sites, log-x.
The selection rule was fixed *before* the curves were drawn: the **largest** threshold whose
sensitivity stays ≥ 0.90 at **both** sites. Largest, because specificity is what is being
bought; both sites, because satisfying only the training hospital is the failure in 04.

## D. The fix

**07 · Re-basing impact.** Sensitivity trades down (0.990 → 0.958 internally) to buy
specificity that did not exist externally at all (0.000 → 0.387). The caveat travels with the
figure: the external dev half *selected* the threshold, so its bars are in-sample and
optimistic.

**08 · PPV vs prevalence.** Sensitivity and specificity survive a change of population; PPV does
not. At a real NICU's ~13% prevalence most positives are false — which is why the product words
a positive as *"have a clinician look"* and never as a diagnosis.

**09 · Calibration.** Internal ECE 0.044, external 0.380. Read the external curve carefully: the
held-out set is 77% positive against 43% internally, so part of the shift is base rate rather
than miscalibration. Either way the app labels this score "calibration unverified".

## E. What the paper argues

**10 · Evaluation unit.** The headline. Identical predictions, scored per image (QWK ≈ 0.52) and
per eye (≈ 0.80), in all four arm-seed cells of the locked test. Nothing about the model changes
between the bars — only the unit the label actually describes.

**11 · Camera view.** *Why* 10 happens, and the check that separates a labelling artefact from a
blind model. ROP grows in the periphery: temporal frames look at it and are missed 12.7% of the
time; posterior-pole frames look away and are missed 71.7%. The errors have an anatomical
direction.

**12 · Confusion, locked test.** The dominant error is the **Normal column**, not the diagonal's
neighbours: 73% of stage-1 frames are called Normal while the Normal row itself is 99% correct.
That asymmetry is the label-unit effect made visible.

**13 · Per-class.** Recall and precision with support annotated. Stage 4/5 (n=4) and AP-ROP
(n=12) are labelled "not externally validated" — a pre-registered decision, not one taken after
seeing the bars.

**14 · Head equivalence.** Structured minus flat at matched sampling: −0.0055 at seed 42,
+0.0224 at seed 1337. **Opposite signs.** A difference that flips direction with the random seed
is noise, and an earlier version of this project reported the seed-42 fold-0 gap as an
architecture win.

**15 · Site probe.** Reported as a negative result. The adversary did not remove site
appearance at any strength. Note the trap the figure makes visible: the *naive* probe scores
lower than the *disease-controlled* one, so an uncontrolled probe understates the leakage it is
supposed to detect.

**16 · Session-unit decomposition.** The familiar AP-ROP headline — recall rising from image
level to session level — conflates *two* effects, and this splits them. **Reweighting**: one
Ostrava infant supplies 76% of AP-ROP images, so weighting units equally moves the number
before any aggregation happens. **Aggregation**: taking the max over a session's frames moves
it again. The right panel is the uncomfortable part — on fold-0 val aggregation dominates
(79%), on the pooled 5-fold split recorded in the same artifact reweighting dominates (70%).
Both are shown, and the paper pins the **pooled split as primary**: it covers every internal
AP-ROP session exactly once, where fold-0 val holds 21 sessions and its decomposition depends
on which fold drew the dominant infant. Fold-0 appears as the instability, not the estimate.

**17 · Grad-CAM attention audit.** Built to answer "does the model read the eye or the
frame?", and the honest answer is that the *total-attention-share* metric cannot settle it.
Swapping the scalar Grad-CAM differentiates moves total off-retina attention by ~25
percentage points on identical weights and identical images, and the served model letterboxes
so a quarter of its frame is synthetic padding that carries no information. Our first
cross-model comparison (40% vs 8%) was measuring those two things, not shortcut reliance.
Area-normalised, both models prefer retina over frame. The metric keeps the job it was built
for — flagging an individual map whose evidence sits off the retina — and loses the job it was
never valid for. What *can* settle the cross-model question is a matched design, and that is
figure 18.

**18 · Matched-objective rerun.** The repair of 17's broken comparison: both models measured
under the same objective form, twice (log-probability of disease, and a raw disease logit).
The peak-outside-retina gap survives both matchings — structured 35.8% vs ResNet50 2.0% under
log-prob, 21.6% vs 2.9% under raw logit — so it is a property of the models, not of the
measurement, and it is reportable. The right panel keeps it honest: under the served
objective, part of the structured model's off-retina peaks land on synthetic letterbox
padding, which the ResNet50's frame cannot exhibit, so the like-for-like number is the
camera-surround share.

## What is deliberately not here

- **No training-curve figure for the served model.** Last-epoch checkpoints are used by protocol;
  a loss curve would invite reading an early-stopping story that did not happen.
- **No ROC for the 6-class staging task.** ROC is a binary instrument; QWK and the confusion
  matrix are the right ones, and 10/12 use them.
- **No figure of Grad-CAM examples as evidence.** Individual heatmaps illustrate, they do not
  measure. The measurement that matters (off-retina attention) ships beside every heatmap in the
  app instead.
