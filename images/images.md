re-running an experiment and re-running the generator keeps the picture and the number in step.

Figures 01 to 12 come from `scripts/make_graphs.py` in our development repository, which reads
`results/*.json`. The two diagrams come from
[../software/make_diagrams.py](../software/make_diagrams.py) in this repository.

## ROP staging figures, `rop_staging/`

These are provisional. Single fold (fold 0), single seed (42). The spread across all five
architectures is smaller than any spread estimate we have, so nothing here supports a ranking. We
will regenerate them once the full 5-fold cross-validation finishes.

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [`00_comparison_macro_f1.png`](rop_staging/00_comparison_macro_f1.png) | All five backbones, in-fold and cross-site, against the metadata-only bar |
| [`00_comparison_per_class_recall.png`](rop_staging/00_comparison_per_class_recall.png) | Per-class recall across models, over the six ICROP classes |
| [`effnetv2s_per_class.png`](rop_staging/effnetv2s_per_class.png) | Precision and recall per class for the best fold-0 backbone |
| [`effnetv2s_training_curves.png`](rop_staging/effnetv2s_training_curves.png) | Loss and validation macro-F1 per epoch |

The most important thing in the comparison figure is the dashed red line at 0.187. That is a
metadata-only baseline which decodes no pixels at all, so a model below it has learned nothing
from the images. All five sit at 0.49 to 0.52, roughly 2.6 times the bar, and that is what lets
us treat any of this as a real result.

The most important thing the figure does not show is a winner. A 0.028 spread across a 7x
parameter range and three model families is noise until the remaining folds land.

Generated by `scripts/make_staging_graphs.py` in our development repository.

## How to read them

Start with 02. It carries the result that most changes how this system should be described:
sensitivity and specificity survive a change of population but PPV does not. ROP catches
essentially every case and is right about 13% of the time it raises a flag in a real NICU. That
is still useful as a triage filter, but it is not what "AUC 0.93" sounds like.

05, 06 and 07 are the same lesson three times over. A pooled number across cameras or collections
is not purely a measure of the disease, because part of it is the model recognising where the
image came from. Every headline in this project is shown with and without that confound.

09 is the argument for routing. The ROP model fires on 59 of 59 adult eyes, and since ROP is a
disease of prematurity and cannot occur in an adult, all 59 are wrong. The model has no way to
abstain from a question outside its world, so routing rather than retraining is the fix.

12 is a negative result and we kept it as one. The metadata we have cannot support a subgroup
fairness claim, and reporting that honestly beats a breakdown built on inadequate data.

## Caveats that travel with these figures

- **Small samples.** The confusion matrices rest on 33 to 40 images. DR's flawless 20/20 has a
  95% interval of [83.2%, 100%], so read it as "at least 83%" rather than "flawless".
- **The flat 100% lines in figure 02** are an artefact of zero false positives on 20 and 18
  negatives, not a guarantee. ROP's curve is the shape to trust.
- **Intervals cluster by patient.** The ROP figures resample infants rather than images, since
  2,411 images come from 29 infants and image-level intervals would be far too narrow.

## On the colours

The original deck palette (cyan, purple, gold, green, red) fails a colourblind check. Red against
green sits at deuteranopia ΔE 6.5, inside the 6 to 8 warn band, and all five hues fall outside the
lightness band for a dark surface.

These figures use validated replacements: DR blue `#3987e5`, ROP orange `#d95926`, Glaucoma aqua
`#199e70`. They pass all six checks with worst adjacent CVD ΔE 9.4.

Colour is never the only cue. Every series is direct-labelled, correct cells in the confusion
matrices carry an outline rather than a different hue, and the impossible cells in figure 09 are
ringed as well as coloured.

## Not in this repository

No patient images. There is no fundus photograph of any real patient here, adult or infant.
During development we found six infant patient photographs being served publicly. Those were
removed, the history was swept and an egress guard was added. Nothing of that kind is in this
repository and nothing of that kind should be added to it.
