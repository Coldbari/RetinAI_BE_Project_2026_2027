## Held separate, and why

| Folder | Images | Why it is not in a stage class |
|---|---:|---|
| `_no_class_label` | 481 | Shantou optic-disc, Shantou vessel and ORVS segmentation sets. Their LabelMe JSON shape labels are `a`/`b` — region names, not disease classes. |
| `_other_pathology` | 1,295 | Ostrava diagnoses that are **not ROP** — hemorrhage, toxoplasma, hamartoms, optic nerve hypoplasia. The current binary model counts all of these as 'ROP Detected'. |
| `_rop_unstaged` | 85 | HVDROPDB ROP images. The source labels them ROP vs Normal only — it has no stage labels, so a stage would have to be invented. |
| `_farfum_plus_disease/` | 1,533 | FARFUM-RoP, grouped by its **plus-disease grade**, which is a different axis from ICROP stage. Its five graders filled the stage column 566/1267/178/366/251 times out of 1,533 — they disagree on whether a stage applies at all — and **zero** of its images are graded unanimously. Merging it into stage_1/2/3 would inject that disagreement into the axis we want to learn. Per-image vote counts are in `MANIFEST.csv`. |

## Caveats that survive the grouping

- **20 Shenzhen images were dropped** as duplicates of higher-resolution ROP-VL originals (same hospital, re-encoded). Matched with a 256-bit perceptual hash calibrated against Shenzhen's own byte-duplicates.
- **Stage 4 (77) and Stage 5 (30) come from one hospital only** — ROP-VL, plus 18 Stage-4 from Multi-View. Any result on these classes is single-site.
- **`stage_0` is not `normal`.** ICROP stage 0 is an immature retina without ROP; it is 45 images from 4 Ostrava infants.
- **`treated` is post-laser / post-anti-VEGF disease**, not active staged ROP. Keep it out of a stage-severity ordinal unless you mean to model it.
- **Patient IDs are namespaced per source** (`ropvl:412`) because they collide across datasets. Always split on `patient_id`, never on the image: 225 ROP-VL infants and 20 Shenzhen infants carry more than one class.
- Filenames are prefixed `<source>__` because HVDROPDB names every file `1.png`..`50.png` and Multi-View, FARFUM and Shantou all use the same UUID.N convention.
